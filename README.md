## 🎯 What is SAHAYAK?

Security and intelligence teams receive information from many different sources:

- 📄 Documents and reports
- 🖼️ Images
- 🎙️ Audio and voice
- 🎥 Video
- 💬 Text and communications
- 📊 Logs and structured data

The real challenge is not simply summarizing these sources.

The challenge is:

> **How do we connect weak signals across different sources without losing the evidence behind them?**

SAHAYAK addresses this problem by converting heterogeneous information into structured intelligence, discovering connections between entities and events, identifying potentially significant cross-source patterns, and allowing analysts to trace findings back to their original evidence.
